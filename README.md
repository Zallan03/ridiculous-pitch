[Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-ridiculous-at-first-glance-real-by-the-second-9a97d12a):
one sentence, at most 200 characters, pitching a bizarre startup idea that sounds
like a joke until the business logic lands.

## The pitch

> We sell museums the smell of history — a Viking longhouse, the Titanic's engine
> room — because visitors forget every plaque but never forget a stink.

Bottling a smell sounds like a joke; museums are a real payer with a real motive —
visitors remember sensory experiences far longer than wall text.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the sentence above to `/output/entry.txt` (Node 22+).
It first gate-checks the text itself: non-empty, a single line, exactly one
sentence, at most 200 characters and 200 bytes, no trailing newline.

```sh
node main.ts
```
